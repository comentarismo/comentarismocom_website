Frequently Asked Questions

How can I log in?

It is simpler than you think. You don't need additional account, sign up with your Facebook, Twitter, Google Plus or GitHub account with only one click.

Can I import my comments from previous / another web site ?

Don't worry! That's our speciality. Send us an email to comentarismobr@gmail.com and include the address of your new and old web site and we will import all the comments for you.

How can I put the comment box under every blog post?

Open the "Settings" page in the Admin Panel and simply copy and paste the code snippet into the source code on every web page you wish to have comment system.The comment box will automatically be inserted.

How do I delete a comment?

In order to delete a comment, you have to log in with your username and navigate to "Comments" panel. There you will find the list of all comments you posted. Click "edit" button for the comment you want to delete and in the new pop up window, click the trash icon.

I upgraded the plan, but I don't see the new features. What can I do?

You may need to refresh or close and re-open your account for the upgraded status to show up.

Can I use comentarismo plugin for my online store?

For sure! Add the snippet to your "Product Page" and start collecting reviews for each of your shop's products.

Have other questions?

Please contact us at comentarismobr@gmail.com. Thank you!
